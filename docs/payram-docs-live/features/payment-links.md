# 🔗Payment Links

Get paid instantly, anywhere.

![](https://docs.payram.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontent.gitbook.com%2Fcontent%2Fwm1DIvbGMREWT1TdLPtz%2Fblobs%2FjQ3yaYhyIE8LZ6sEftC7%2Fpayram-feature-payment-links.png&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=9e39b231&sv=2)

Payment Links are shareable URLs that let customers pay you in seconds.

No checkout page or integration required.

Simply Create ➡️ Share Link ➡️ Collect Payments.

Close sales directly in chats, DMs, or emails. Perfect for one-off sales, custom invoices, or quick payments. Less friction. More conversions.

* * *

### **Why it matters**[](#why-it-matters)

-   **Faster checkout:** Customers pay in one click, no redirects or logins.
    
-   **Flexible use:** Great for one-time transactions, services, or quick settlements.
    
-   **Seamless sharing:** Works across WhatsApp, Telegram, email, or any platform.
    
-   **Higher conversions:** Remove barriers and capture intent instantly.
    
-   **Universal access:** Works on all devices and browsers, with built-in security.
    

* * *

### How to create a payment link[](#how-to-create-a-payment-link)

1

### Creating payment link[](#creating-payment-link)

Go to Payments and expand the section. Select Create Payment Link to generate a new payment link.

![](https://docs.payram.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontent.gitbook.com%2Fcontent%2Fwm1DIvbGMREWT1TdLPtz%2Fblobs%2FW7nvMGOVDGosaWZkXe98%2Fimage.png&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=dba55c35&sv=2)

2

### How to add a member[](#how-to-add-a-member)

Before you select **Generate Payment Link**, complete these steps:

1.  Add a new member by entering their email, or select an existing member if available.
    
2.  If this is your first time setting up, the member list is likely empty.
    

3

### Add new member[](#add-new-member)

Select the member email input box. Select Add New Member when the option appears. A pop-up screen opens where you can enter the member’s details.

![](https://docs.payram.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontent.gitbook.com%2Fcontent%2Fwm1DIvbGMREWT1TdLPtz%2Fblobs%2FwMxs4Pv1ktO6KrFjRSV2%2Fimage.png&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=19e5b386&sv=2)

Enter the customer’s email, select the project, and then select Add Member.

![](https://docs.payram.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontent.gitbook.com%2Fcontent%2Fwm1DIvbGMREWT1TdLPtz%2Fblobs%2FwFhwUdCPDt945SR6rBcN%2Fimage.png&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=c10c7948&sv=2)

4

### Enter amount[](#enter-amount)

After you add the member details, enter the amount to charge that user. Select Generate Payment Link. The system generates a link, which you share with your customer.

![](https://docs.payram.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontent.gitbook.com%2Fcontent%2Fwm1DIvbGMREWT1TdLPtz%2Fblobs%2FHDFN51U7ybBMqGYtg4ta%2Fimage.png&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=f11a4dc5&sv=2)

5

### Click on generate payment link[](#click-on-generate-payment-link)

After you add the member details, enter the amount to charge that user. Select Generate Payment Link. The system generates a link, which you share with your customer.

![](https://docs.payram.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontent.gitbook.com%2Fcontent%2Fwm1DIvbGMREWT1TdLPtz%2Fblobs%2FDRS2R4oW1t5lJ5Aw1UcY%2Fimage.png&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=3f2c567a&sv=2)

6

### Payment page[](#payment-page)

You'll be redirected to a payment link you can share that to your customer.

![](https://docs.payram.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontent.gitbook.com%2Fcontent%2Fwm1DIvbGMREWT1TdLPtz%2Fblobs%2FMCOKvi0v1jeoJSZK8TKX%2Fimage.png&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=3e893cc5&sv=2)

**Note**: If you generate a payment link and the deposit address appears blank, it means the blocks are not being processed. To fix this, restart your PayRam server by running the reset command script.

👉 [View Restart Command Guide](/script/script-usage)

7

### Select preferred coin and network[](#select-preferred-coin-and-network)

When making a payment, customers can select their preferred coin and network.

![](https://docs.payram.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontent.gitbook.com%2Fcontent%2Fwm1DIvbGMREWT1TdLPtz%2Fblobs%2FGkLBgFo8Gv1ajmCGRaVB%2Fimage.png&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=1ad0098f&sv=2)

Currently, PayRam supports these coins and networks.

8

### Payment successful[](#payment-successful)

After the customer pays, the status updates once the minimum number of onchain confirmations are complete. When confirmed, the system shows Payment Successful.

![](https://docs.payram.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcontent.gitbook.com%2Fcontent%2Fwm1DIvbGMREWT1TdLPtz%2Fblobs%2FgIpNDc1Fq8cSnk7D26iy%2Fimage.png&width=768&dpr=3&quality=100&sign=a34073e1&sv=2)

### **Sharing links**[](#sharing-links)

Share links wherever your customers are.

You can:

-   Copy and paste directly into chats or emails.
    
-   Use the **Share via** menu to send through WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS.
    
-   Embed inside invoices, receipts, or marketing campaigns.
    

> Pro tip: Combine with QR codes for offline payments: Scan, Pay, Done.

* * *

### **Tracking payments**[](#tracking-payments)

Every link comes with built-in analytics:

-   Payment status (Pending, Successful, Cancelled)
    
-   Customer details
    
-   Amount received, network details, and time of payment
    

View all active and completed links under **Payments → All Payments**.

* * *

### **Common use cases**[](#common-use-cases)

-   **Freelancers:** Get paid for projects or hourly services.
    
-   **Businesses:** Send quick pay links for invoices or order confirmations.
    
-   **Support teams:** Collect payments inside chat tickets.
    
-   **Events:** Share payment links for registrations or donations.
    
-   **Influencers & creators:** Sell digital goods directly via DMs.
