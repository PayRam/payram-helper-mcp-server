# Supported Networks and Coins

Your customers can pay in three ways: send crypto directly, pay from a chain PayRam bridges, or pay by card. Each supports a different set of assets.

* * *

## Pay with crypto[](#pay-with-crypto)

Coins your customers can send straight to a PayRam deposit address.

Network

Coins

**Ethereum**

ETH, USDC, USDT, PYUSD, cbBTC

**Base**

ETH, USDC, cbBTC

**Polygon**

POL, USDC, USDT

**Tron**

TRX, USDT

**Bitcoin**

BTC

**Note**: A coin only appears at checkout once you have enabled it for the project and deployed the deposit wallet for that network. See [Wallet Integration](/onboarding-guide/wallet-integration).

* * *

## Pay from another chain[](#pay-from-another-chain)

PayRam can accept payment on a chain it does not settle on. Your customer pays on the origin chain, a bridge provider converts it, and the funds arrive at your normal deposit address.

Pay on

Coins accepted

You receive

**Solana**

USDC, USDT, PYUSD, SOL

USDC on Base

**Note**: Bridged payments are available on mainnet only, and only when your project accepts USDC on Base. Nothing extra to configure — no Solana wallet, node or deposit address is involved.

* * *

## Pay by card[](#pay-by-card)

Customers without crypto can pay by card, wallet or bank transfer, and you still settle in crypto. PayRam offers three methods, each enabled separately and each delivering to a different set of chains.

**Note**: Card methods deliver on mainnet. On testnet, stablecoin purchases cannot be completed. See [Card-to-Crypto Fiat Onramp](/features/card-to-crypto-fiat-onramp).
